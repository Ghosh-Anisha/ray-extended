# Phase U6 decisions

- **U3 was blocked, so My dashboard did not exist.** U6 builds it in full (3.6 and 12) on `src/surfaces/dashboard.ts` and `src/ui/dashboard/`. Ask RAY, Rules and the other designs are untouched; "Ask about this widget" only links to `/ask?q=...`.
- **One new table, `ui_state`** (migration `1300_ui_state`): a JSON value per merchant and key for the dashboard setup, widgets, asks, schedules and the lever cards. Nothing a guard or case reads lives there. If another phase also adds a migration, keep both entries in `_journal.json` and renumber the `idx`.
- **Seed:** `DEMO_SEED_VERSION` is 2. `src/db/seed/demo-dashboard.ts` adds six unpaid carts above ₹5,000 (orders with no payment, so the 94.2% success rate and the case counts do not move) and six past asks (success rate four times this week).
- **Watch:** a pinned widget is compared with the same hour of earlier days. With fewer than two earlier weekdays of the same name (the demo has one Tuesday) it uses the seven days before, and says so ("Compared with the days before"). Success rate is watched by method; sales by total. The action opens the open case that holds one of the failed payments.
- **Starter pins:** RAY watches what the merchant says they check every day, so Success rate starts unpinned. The "You asked about success rate 4 times this week. Pin it?" card then pins it and the Unusual badge appears.
- **"By bank":** the payment record has no bank, so the widget groups by UPI app (from the handle) and cards, and says so in its definition.
- **Reports:** there is no settlement feed. Settlements are captured payments grouped by day, with an assumed 2% fee and 18% GST, stated in the summary. Scheduling to the CA on WhatsApp is Simulated and only saves a schedule.
- **Cardless EMI:** the ₹ estimate assumes 15% of unpaid big carts finish with EMI, shown on the card. Turn on is Simulated and records the numbers at that moment; two weeks later on the fake clock the card shows before and after, where "after" is a simulated 30% fewer unpaid carts. No fee percentage is quoted because the prototype has none.
- **Try it:** the five moments and Grow open a coach-mark tour (`?tour=`). Designs built by other sessions link to their routes (`/cases?moment=whose_side`, `ticket`, `settlement_hold`, `ai_chat_order`, `/dues`). No capability is "Coming soon" yet; the chip exists for later.
- **Coach marks:** `src/ui/coach/`. A screen sequence shows once per browser (`ray-coach:<screen>` in localStorage); "Show me around" in the account menu clears them and replays. Steps whose element is missing are skipped. The shell mounts `CoachHost` (two small edits to `shell.tsx`).
- `src/db/seed.test.ts` lists every table; `ui_state: 0` was added to its expected rows (the base seed leaves it empty). Nothing was loosened.
